example_sql = "SELECT c.id, COUNT(o.id) FROM customers c LEFT JOIN orders o ON c.id=o.customer_id AND o.status='paid' GROUP BY c.id ORDER BY c.id;"

外连接先根据 ON 匹配行，再为无匹配项补上右表 NULL。WHERE 在此之后过滤。将仅用于限定匹配的右表条件放入 ON，可以保留未匹配的左表行。
